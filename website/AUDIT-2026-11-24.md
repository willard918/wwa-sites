## Needs your decision
1. **Licensing wording.** Footer shows an Oregon licence only (ARI-13396) but the site markets WA and CA and the lot check covers them. Confirm you may offer architectural services there (or add the licence numbers / a partner architect), or limit the claims to Oregon.
2. **Contact form needs email delivery before launch.** A contact form (POST /api/contact, added 2026-11-19 in the other session) is live, but the server has no SMTP settings, so messages only land in `/var/lib/wwa/notify` on the VPS and nobody is told, while the page tells the visitor "We got your message". Set `WWA_SMTP_HOST/PORT/USER/PASS`, `WWA_NOTIFY` and `WWA_POSTAL` in `/etc/wwa.env` (a mailbox app password at HostGator or a transactional provider) and test it, or keep the mailto link only, before the DNS switch.
3. **Analytics.** None. Suggest a privacy-friendly counter (Plausible or Cloudflare Web Analytics) so you can see letter scans, lot checks and quote clicks.
4. **Terms page.** The old site had Terms and Conditions; the new one has Privacy only.
5. **Prices:** the other session re-enabled a Design & Kit estimate ($55/sf kit + $15,000 design, both marked unconfirmed). Confirm those numbers with Pacific Wall / Trillium before the public launch, or hide them again.
6. **Photos.** The site uses rendered images only. Real project photos would build trust (Studio section).
7. **Test letters:** I filled the zip as 97504 without a source; the geocoder returned 97501 for 2070 Skyview Dr. Verify the zip before mailing.

## Known weaknesses (lower priority)
- First paint of the hero is dark for ~1-2 s while the 3D render builds.
- three.js and Google Fonts load from third-party hosts (privacy and availability); could be self-hosted.
- The page is one long scroll (~16,800 px); consider a short "how it works" section and a FAQ for search (permits, financing, timeline, who builds).
- No testimonials or case study; the old project gallery looked like template filler and was not carried over.
- Lot check covers Jackson, Curry, Klamath, Lake (OR), WA, and 7 CA counties; setbacks are assumptions and city zoning is not connected.
