On site

From truck to roof.

The wall panels and roof cassettes arrive numbered and are set by a telescopic-boom machine. Press Watch it go up in the configurator to see it, then look around the site below and check the boom reach for the unit you chose.

Engineering

Every wall, numbered.

Each wall is split into factory panels, and every panel carries its own ID from the model to the fabricator to the site. Switch the configurator to Panels to see them.

  • Panelised walls. Openings get their own panel, with king studs either side.
  • One model. The same parameters drive the drawings, the panel schedule and the take-off.
  • No guesswork on site. Panels arrive labeled and in order.
