Privacy

In plain language. Last updated 2026-11-10.

What we collect

  • If you ask us to email you a design: your email address, the design you chose, and the property address if you add one.
  • If you check a lot: the address you type is sent to our server, which looks it up in public records (the US Census geocoder and the county parcel map). We keep a short-lived copy of the result so a repeat lookup is quick.
  • If you arrive from a letter: the code printed on it, so we can show you your own lot.
  • If you click a link in our email: that you clicked, and which "when are you thinking?" answer you tapped. Your IP address is turned into a short scrambled code so we can count repeat visits; we do not store the address itself.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your details, and we do not add you to a mailing list. A design request sends you one email.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.
  • We do not read owner names from the county records.

Who else sees it

  • Our web host and email provider handle the data on our behalf.
  • Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and the 3D library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, so those services see that your browser asked for them.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it. To unsubscribe, use the link in any email from us or reply "stop". To have everything we hold about you deleted, write to willardw@willardwilliamsarchitect.com.

About the lot check

It is a first look from public data, with assumed setbacks, and it is not a zoning determination or a survey. Building outlines come from satellite-derived data that can be out of date.
